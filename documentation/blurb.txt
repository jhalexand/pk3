Pong Kombat 3
-------------

This game is a duplication of the original game of Pong Kombat 3.  It
is a super-hyped variation on the orginal and very simple game of
pong, where two players each can move a paddle around, and try to
bounce a ball back and forth between them.  It plays off the
one-on-one fighting arcade game of Mortal Kombat and uses many of the
characters and motifs first seen there.

In Pong Kombat 3, each player chooses a paddle, where each paddle has
a different character and different abilities associated with it.  The
object of the game is to eliminate all of one's opponents by either
bouncing the ball past one's opponents, or simply by going up to them
and fighting it out one-on-one, and utilizing the paddle's special
capabilities.


This Version
------------
This version of Pong Kombat 3 is networked and can have 1 to 4 players
battling on 2 teams.  There are 6 different paddles/players to choose
from, each with their own special abilities.

Upcoming Features
-----------------
Upcoming features include being able to fight against paddles with
Artificial Intelligence.
