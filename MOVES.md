# Pong Kombat 3 — Moves

## Kontrols

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← ↑ ↓ → | Move |
| A | Punch (5 damage) |
| S | Kick (5 damage) |
| D | Block |
| P / U | Pause / Unpause |
| Hold ↑ or ↓ + Punch | Uppercut (10 damage) |
| Hold back + Kick | Sweep (10 damage) |

Blocking cuts damage to a tenth, so a blocked punch or kick does nothing.

## Special moves

Arrows are for the left side. On the right side, swap ← and →.

| Fighter | Move | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| Cyrax | Net, holds the opponent 5 s | ← ← Kick |
| Cyrax | Bomb, blows up at the far wall after 2 s | Hold Punch, then → → Kick (or ← ← Kick) |
| Liu Kang | Fireball | → → Punch |
| Liu Kang | Flying kick | → → Kick |
| Liu Kang | Bicycle kick | Hold Kick 1.5 s, then release |
| Jade | Projectile | ← → Punch (fires on press) |
| Jade | Three-way spread | ← ← → → Punch (fires on press) |
| Jade | Projectile shield, 4 s | ← → Kick |
| Kabal | Projectile | ← ← Punch |
| Kabal | Spin dash, stuns the opponent | ← → Kick |
| Kabal | Buzzsaw that climbs the opponent's wall | ← ← ← Block |
| Reptile | Acid spit | → → Punch |
| Reptile | Invisible until hit | ↑ ↑ ↓ Kick |
| Sub-Zero | Ice ball, freezes 5 s | ↓ → Punch |
| Sub-Zero | Slow homing ice ball | → ↓ ← Punch |

## How combos register

- Tap each arrow: press and release it. The game records keys when you let go of them.
- Most specials fire when you release the final Punch or Kick. Moves marked "fires on press" go off when you press it, and Kabal's buzzsaw fires when you press Block.
- The whole sequence must come within half a second of that final key. Liu Kang gets 1 second.
- Tapping arrows nudges your paddle a little; that's normal.
- The plain Punch or Kick still happens when you press it, before the special fires.
- You can't use a special while blocking, frozen, netted or stunned.

## Goals

The ball hitting your wall takes 10 life from you. A round ends when a player's life reaches zero. The first player to win two rounds wins the match.
